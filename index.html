Africa Route Quest

Connect the Continent!

How to play

  1. 📍Location card: find the country on the map and choose its capital. Correct = 2 points. If you miss, the next team can steal for 1 point.
  2. 🎲Roll the die and travel along the routes: 1–2 = 1 route, 3–4 = up to 2, 5–6 = up to 3.
  3. ⚡Challenge card at your new hub: earn the points printed on the card (1–3).
  4. 🛤️Claim the route you travelled: name the two countries and the border(s) it crosses for 2 bonus points.
  5. 🏁The game ends when every team has answered 6 challenge cards or the timer runs out. Highest score wins!

Before every answer: check the map first! Someone who didn't choose the answer must explain it.
